# Deployment Checklist - Lakshmi Landing

## Pre-deployment

- [x] Contact flow uses direct phone and email only
- [x] Homepage CTA points to contact options
- [x] Contact page has no form
- [x] Script does not depend on form elements
- [x] Service pages with placeholders are still labeled clearly

## Deployment

1. Verify the landing page opens correctly.
2. Tap the phone link on mobile.
3. Open the email link on desktop and mobile.
4. Confirm the contact section is visible and readable.
5. Check navigation and service pages.

## Post-launch

- Confirm phone and email links work in production.
- Confirm the footer contact block is visible.
- Check that no old form instructions remain in user-facing copy.
